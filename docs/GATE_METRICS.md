# Gate Metrics — S1 Preflight

> **IMPORTANT: All thresholds are PROVISIONAL.**  
> They were calibrated on **synthetic scenes only** (generated by `scripts/make_synthetic_scene.py`)
> and have **NOT been validated on real 360° footage**.  
> Once real sample videos are available, each threshold must be re-calibrated and the
> status updated from **provisional** to **calibrated**.

---

## 1. Format / Aspect-Ratio Check

| Check | Rule | Rejection Code |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | width/height must be in `[1.9, 2.1]` | `not_equirect` |
| Square input | If `0.9 ≤ aspect ≤ 1.1` AND `stereo_policy=reject` | `stereo_unsupported` |

**Rationale**: Equirectangular video is defined by a 2:1 aspect ratio. Anything
significantly outside this range is likely a flat, cubemap, or fisheye format that
the SfM pipeline cannot handle directly.  The 1:1 check catches Top/Bottom stereo
(VR180/3D360), which would require separating the two eyes before processing.

---

## 2. Duration Check

| Metric | Default threshold | Status | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Segment duration | ≥ `min_segment_seconds` (5.0 s) | provisional | `too_short` |
| Segment duration | ≤ `max_segment_seconds` (900.0 s) | provisional | (none yet) |

**Rationale**: Fewer than ~5 seconds provides too few frames to establish camera
parallax for SfM. Very long single-scene segments are also suspicious and may be
a mis-detection.

---

## 3. Blur

| Metric | Formula | Default threshold | Status | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Per-frame blur | Variance of Laplacian on a rig view | `blur_threshold` = 50.0 | provisional | — |
| Blur fraction | Fraction of sampled frames below `blur_threshold` | ≤ `max_blur_fraction` = 0.60 | provisional | `excess_blur` |

**Rationale**: Blurry frames are caused by fast motion or camera shake. When the
majority of frames are blurry the SfM feature extractor will struggle to find
repeatable keypoints. The variance-of-Laplacian is computed on rig (perspective)
views rather than on the raw equirect to avoid equatorial-distortion bias.

---

## 4. Optical-Flow Baseline and Measurement Design

### Why a baseline instead of adjacent frames?

Flow computed between adjacent frames at native FPS (~1/30 s gap) is dominated
by sensor noise and rendering quantisation artefacts at the output resolutions
used for preflight (320 × 160 px).  The true translational signal is too small
to be reliably separated from the rotational component via least-squares.

By measuring flow between frames separated by `flow_baseline_seconds` (default
0.5 s) we increase the translational displacement by ~15× relative to the noise
floor, making the rotation / translation decomposition reliable.

**Adjacent-pair measurement** (1/fps gap) is still performed, but only for the
`motion_too_fast` check, where _total_ flow magnitude is what matters.

### Rotation / Translation Separation

For each sampled frame-pair (at the baseline interval) we compute dense
Farnebäck optical flow on each perspective rig view.  We then fit a global
angular-velocity vector `ω` by solving:

```
[J_u_world; J_v_world] @ ω ≈ [flow_u; flow_v]
```

via least-squares across all rig views simultaneously.  The residual after
subtracting the predicted rotational flow is the translational (parallax)
component.

## 5. Parallax / Camera Motion

| Metric | Formula | Default threshold | Status | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Median residual flow | Median of `‖flow_residual‖` over baseline pairs | ≥ `min_flow_magnitude` = 1.5 px | provisional | `no_parallax` |
| Rotation ratio | `‖rot_flow‖ / (‖rot_flow‖ + ‖res_flow‖)` | ≤ `max_rotation_ratio` = 0.35 | provisional | `rotation_dominated` |

> **Note on rotation ratio threshold**: The value 0.35 was derived from measurements on
> 320×160 px synthetic scenes with a procedural checkerboard cube room.  Real-world footage
> at higher resolution may give different values; this threshold **must** be re-calibrated.
> Measured separation on synthetics: pass=0.099, pure_yaw=0.425, gap = 0.326.

**`no_parallax`**: Scenes with essentially no camera translation (static tripod,
nodal-point spinning) have near-zero residual flow.  Such clips contain no depth
information and 3DGS / SfM will either fail or produce degenerate reconstructions.

**`rotation_dominated`**: Pure yaw/pitch/roll sequences can produce high total
flow, but the rotational component accounts for nearly all of it.  A rotation
ratio close to 1.0 indicates insufficient translational baseline for triangulation.

## 6. Motion Speed

| Metric | Formula | Default threshold | Status | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjacent-pair median flow | Median total flow between consecutive native frames | ≤ `max_flow_magnitude` = 2.0 px | provisional | `motion_too_fast` |

> Threshold calibrated on 320×160 px at 10 fps: `pass` adj=0.76 px, `too_fast` adj=2.60 px.
> Must be re-calibrated for real footage at higher resolution and different FPS.

**Rationale**: Very fast camera motion causes motion blur and insufficient
feature overlap between frames, both of which degrade SfM.  This is classified
as `PASS_WITH_WARNINGS` (not `REJECT`) because the `ride_fast` preset can
mitigate it through denser keyframe selection.

---

## 7. Exposure Stability

| Metric | Formula | Default threshold | Status | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exposure flicker | Mean `|Δ luminance|` across sampled frames | ≤ `exposure_flicker_threshold` = 50.0 | provisional | `exposure_unstable` |

**Rationale**: Rapid auto-exposure changes between frames produce inconsistent
appearance across keyframes, which corrupts photometric consistency assumptions
in 3DGS training and can also confuse feature matching in SfM.

---

## 8. Scene Splitting

Scenes are detected with **PySceneDetect** (`AdaptiveDetector`) or supplied
explicitly via `--scene-timestamps`.  Each scene becomes an independent child
job (own workspace and manifest).  The parent job summarises all children.

User-supplied `--scene-timestamps` **always override** auto-detection.
